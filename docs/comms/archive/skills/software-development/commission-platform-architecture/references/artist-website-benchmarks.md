# Artist Personal Website Benchmarks

> Collected 2026-07. Actual websites with real URL access and page analysis.
> Filter: beautiful, not bloated, not dated, not off-putting.

## Top Picks

### 1. Clara Fois — clarafois.com
- **Stack**: Custom (Webflow-style), **~49KB**
- **Colors**: `#FF342A20` red accent + white/light-grey
- **Layout**: Single-page scroll: #about → #projects → #services → #process → Contact
- **Best detail**: Process section visualizes the commission workflow as steps. SaaS-style page for an illustrator — highly distinctive.
- **For**: Commission platforms should offer Process-as-a-component.

### 2. Joana Neves — joananevesart.com
- **Stack**: Framer, **~165KB** (104 lines of HTML!)
- **Colors**: `#fff9ed` warm-white + `#03332a` dark-green + `#cf3946` accent-red
- **Layout**: Hero → grid gallery → client logos → inline email contact
- **Best detail**: Three-color constraint throughout the entire page. Email is plain text at the bottom ("email@…"), no contact form.
- **For**: Ultra-lightweight template reference.

### 3. Titus Lunter — tituslunter.com
- **Stack**: Squarespace, **~79KB**
- **Colors**: Dark bg (`#111`/`#000`), white text, artwork provides all color
- **Layout**: Full-screen slideshow of works, navigation in corner (6 links)
- **Best detail**: Zero thumbnail-grid abstraction — each piece gets the full viewport.
- **For**: Concept/game artist template archetype.

## Common Design Patterns Across Top Performers

1. **Dark background** — not universal but overwhelmingly preferred for art portfolios (makes colors pop)
2. **Navigation ≤ 6 links** — never competes with visual content
3. **Price open on cards** — "DM for price" is friction, reduces inquiries
4. **Commission status badge** — OPEN/CLOSED in accent color, top of page
5. **Inline contact info** — email as text, not behind a form wall
6. **Will Draw / Won't Draw lists** — explicit scope reduces back-and-forth
7. **Mobile-first** — most traffic arrives from social media share links on phones
